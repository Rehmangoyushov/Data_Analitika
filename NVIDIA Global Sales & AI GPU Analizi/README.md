## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive, multi-page *Power BI* dashboard designed to analyze Nvidia's global sales, costs, and profit performance. The project involves data cleaning, modeling, and transforming complex data into actionable business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* *Total Gross Sales:* 433bn
* *Total Net Sales:* 48bn
* *Total Cost:* 36bn
* *Sum of Profit:* 665bn

---

## 🚀 Analytical Insights & Visuals

* *Product Performance:* The majority of net sales are driven by the *H100 AI GPU* (257bn) and *A100 AI GPU* (138bn).
* *Geographical Performance:* The highest net sales by country are generated in Saudi Arabia (45bn), Argentina (44bn), and the USA (44bn). Total costs are evenly distributed across regions (~20% each).
* *Trend Analysis (Line & Waterfall Charts):* Monthly net sales dynamics and cumulative growth trends are analyzed to track performance over time.
* *Advanced Analytics:* A Decomposition Tree is utilized to break down profit by region, and a Scatter Plot is implemented to visualize the correlation between total cost and profit by product and region.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* *Power BI* (DAX, Power Query, Data Modeling)
* *SQL / Python* (For initial data preprocessing and cleaning)
